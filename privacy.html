TataBot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TataBot ("TataBot", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use or interact with TataBot on Discord.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled on a Discord server, TataBot may process limited information necessary to provide its functionality. This may include:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord message information required for configured logging features
  • Discord commands and interactions sent to TataBot
  • Server configuration information
  • Webhook information and data sent through configured webhooks
  • Online-player information when the relevant server-status feature is used

TataBot does not intentionally request or store Discord passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card information, or other Discord account credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by TataBot may be used for the following purposes:

  • Operating and providing TataBot's features
  • Processing Discord commands and interactions
  • Providing server logging functionality
  • Processing configured webhook events
  • Providing server status and online-player information
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse
  • Diagnosing technical problems and errors
  • Maintaining and improving TataBot

3. Discord

TataBot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

TataBot does not control Discord's collection or processing of information. For information about Discord's own data practices, please refer to Discord's official privacy documentation.

4. Webhooks and Third-Party Services

TataBot may interact with third-party services when those services are configured by a server administrator.

For example, server administrators may configure webhooks to receive notifications or logging information. Information sent to such services may be processed according to the privacy policies of those third-party services.

TataBot does not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant TataBot feature, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

The exact retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

6. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe TataBot has stored information associated with you and you would like that information reviewed or deleted, contact us using the contact information below.

When submitting a request, please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

7. Server Administrators

Server owners and administrators are responsible for configuring TataBot and deciding which logging, webhook, or other features are enabled on their Discord server.

If a server administrator configures TataBot to process or forward information through a third-party service, that processing may also be subject to the administrator's own policies and the third party's privacy policy.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by TataBot. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

TataBot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

Your continued use of TataBot after an updated Privacy Policy is published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.
